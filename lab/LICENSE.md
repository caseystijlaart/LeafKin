# Lab test photos

160 photos used only to compare photo health checks in the beta's Lab page. None were used to train LeafKin's model.
They are resized copies (longest side 320 px) from:

- Aloe Vera Leaf Disease Detection Dataset: Khan, Reza, Mojumdar et al., Mendeley Data, doi 10.17632/7w6t4zx33n.1, CC BY 4.0
- Cactus dataset: Benali & Jdey, Mendeley Data, doi 10.17632/jgt7ghdmg5.1, CC BY 4.0
- Indoor Plant Disease Image Dataset: Mahmud Opu, Islam, Abdullah & Akter (ICCIT 2025), Kaggle/Mendeley, CC BY 4.0
- Indoor Plant Leaf Health Dataset: Gaurav Saklani, Kaggle, CC BY-SA 4.0 (the "other plants" photos; this folder's
  copies of them are shared under the same licence)

Combined on Kaggle by Abdul Ahad ("Indoor Plant Disease Detection Dataset", CC BY 4.0).
